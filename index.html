12 Scoops of Christmas, Vanilla Bean Creamery

Thank you so much for your interest in our first-ever 12 Scoops of Christmas Advent Calendar! We’re so excited to share this with you and hope it becomes a fun holiday activity for the whole family.

Each box is $59.95 and includes 12 surprise ice cream flavors, each in a 4 oz. cup (similar to our Kiddie Cup size), 4 toppings, and a custom scratch-off card. The fun part: taste each flavor first, have your family guess what it is, and then scratch it off to reveal the answer!

A few important details

  • Unfortunately, we are unable to accommodate flavor substitutions or special requests for these boxes. The back of your scratch-off card will include allergen information for each flavor so you can review it before tasting.
  • Calendars are in-store pickup only; delivery is not available. Payment will be collected when you pick up your box.
  • Please note that all calendars must be picked up by the end of the day on December 12. Any boxes that have not been picked up by then will be released from hold and made available to other customers.

Quantities are limited, so please fill out the form below as soon as possible to reserve yours!
